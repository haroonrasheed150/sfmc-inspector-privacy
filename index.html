SFMC Collect Event Inspector

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Publisher: Haroon Rasheed

What the extension does

This independent developer QA tool displays events sent by a configured storefront to nova.collect.igodigital.com. It does not send events or modify storefront requests.

Information handled

After you review the in-product disclosure and click Start, the extension reads matching Collect event names, account IDs, and parsed payloads. Payloads can contain customer email addresses or IDs, page and search details, product and cart data, prices, and other fields sent by the storefront. It also reads the igodigitaltc2 cookie when available and may read that cookie from the outgoing Collect request header.

How information is used and stored

Information is used only to display and export events for local implementation QA. The extension stores up to 500 event payloads and a current cookie identifier in Chrome local extension storage. Data remains on your device until you clear it or remove the extension. The extension does not upload this information to the publisher or another server. The storefront may independently send its configured Collect events to Salesforce; the extension only observes those requests.

Sharing

The extension does not share or sell collected information. If you export event data, the file is created on your device and is under your control. The popup has an optional PayPal.Me link; clicking it opens PayPal directly. The extension does not handle payment information. PayPal processes any payment under its own terms and privacy policy.

Your controls

Click Stop to stop collection and remove the floating panel. Uncheck the consent box to revoke consent, stop collection, and clear the extension's event history and cached identifiers. Click Clear events to remove the same stored history while collection is active. Neither action deletes the cookie from the storefront's cookie store. You can remove the extension's optional site and cookie permissions in Chrome settings or uninstall the extension.

Permissions

The extension requests access only to storefront hosts you enter and to the relevant igodigital.com host for the Collect event and cookie feature. It uses Chrome's tabs and scripting APIs to show or remove the floating panel, the webRequest API to inspect the specified Collect event and cookie header, and the cookies API to query igodigitaltc2.

Chrome Web Store Limited Use

Our use of information received through Chrome APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. We use information only to provide the disclosed event-inspection feature, do not sell it, and do not transfer it to advertising or data-broker services.
